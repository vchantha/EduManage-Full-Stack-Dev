Quadratic Functions: Practice

Name: __________________________   Date: _______________

  1. Complete a value table for f(x) = x² − 4 for x = −2, −1, 0, 1, 2.
  2. Find the vertex and axis of symmetry of y = x² − 6x + 8.
  3. Solve x² + 3x − 10 = 0 by factorization.
  4. Solve 2x² − 8 = 0.
  5. A rectangle has area 48 square units. Its length is two units longer than its width. Write and solve a quadratic equation to find its dimensions.
Teacher answer key

1. 0, −3, −4, −3, 0.

2. Vertex (3, −1); axis x = 3.

3. x = 2 or x = −5.

4. x = 2 or x = −2.

5. w(w + 2) = 48; w² + 2w − 48 = 0; dimensions 6 by 8.
