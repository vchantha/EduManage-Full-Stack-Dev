Quadratic Functions

A four-period lesson guide for Algebra and Functions.

Learning objectives

  • Recognize and describe a quadratic function and its graph.
  • Identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and intercepts.
  • Solve quadratic equations using factorization and the quadratic formula.
f(x) = ax² + bx + c, where a ≠ 0

1. Explore the graph

Sketch y = x² using the points (−2, 4), (−1, 1), (0, 0), (1, 1), and (2, 4). The graph forms a parabola. Compare it with y = −x² and y = 2x².

2. Locate the vertex

The axis of symmetry is x = −b/(2a). Substitute that x value into f(x) to find the vertex’s y coordinate.

3. Solve a quadratic equation

For x² − 5x + 6 = 0, factor to obtain (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0. The solutions are x = 2 and x = 3.

4. Check for understanding

Ask students to explain how the value of a changes the opening direction of the parabola, then solve x² − 9 = 0 independently.
